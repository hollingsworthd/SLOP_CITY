# SLOP CITY™ — v1.0.1 source

**Current source: v1.0.1.** The city has 40 distinct physical job mechanics, a persistent salvage inventory and an indoor pawn shop at Signal Yard. Dust-prone objects visibly polish over four CLEAN actions. Ordinary props, exterior window panes and wall panels can be recovered and sold; floors, elevators, video billboards and posters stay in place. The city includes 204 additional protected posters.

There are **1,984 Agent Smiths and 42 women in red**: 33 on street routes and nine in separate buildings. Residents use a stable logical registry and bounded nearby simulation/rendering, rather than thousands of simultaneously active bodies. The six-stop automatic subway and eight driveable cars remain.

**New controls:** `C` cleans, `F` takes an aimed object, and `I` opens/closes the bag. Touch controls provide **CLEAN**, **TAKE**, and **BAG**. Aim at the physical counter inside Signal Yard and use `E` / **USE** to sell or buy back individual items. Opening the bag away from the counter does not sell anything.

Builds use the pinned Unity 6000.3.26f1 editor. This source version does not relabel previously delivered Mac binaries. Current verification and publication details are recorded in the workspace `AGENTS.md` and `Unity/Builds/Reports/world-work-20261016/` (or `Builds/Reports/world-work-20261016/` from this Unity directory). Bounded scene tests are not a physical-iPhone endurance or universal crash-recovery claim.

## Play

Choose **Play**, press **E** to stand, and open the home door. Your current contract stays at the upper left. Small white-rabbit ground stencils mark accessible passages, while intercepted messages name the district, landmark and action. Press **T** (or mobile **SIGNAL**) to replay the hint. Clues identify doors, lifts and transit entrances when the destination is on another level. Forty typed jobs generate continuously, with two to five stages and reusable devices. Complete the work to record its outcome and receive another dispatch. Ordinary jobs grant no money or XP. **Q** swaps a contract for free.

Use **WASD** to move, **mouse** to look, **Shift** to run, **Space** to jump, **E** to interact, **R** to return home, **M** for media, **H** to hide/show the HUD, and **Esc** to pause. Aim at an object before using it. Cars use **W/S** to accelerate/brake/reverse, **A/D** to steer, and **E** to exit. Use marked lifts for rooftop decks and subway platforms; board a train and use its ride console to travel between stations.

On phones and tablets, touch the **left side** for a floating movement stick and drag the **right side** to look. Tap **USE**, **JUMP**, **MEDIA**, **JOB**, **HOME** or **PAUSE**; hold **RUN** to sprint. Movement and looking work together with independent fingers. Landscape gives more room; portrait, screen rotation and display safe areas are supported. Touch browsers start at the 540p render setting.

Bins and parts crates supply usable components; repairs consume them. Free public vending supplies parts, water and a movement boost. Fountains and hydrants refill water for plant care. Recycling becomes replacement parts, and field readings can be filed at municipal terminals. Benches offer rest, lamps switch actual illumination, and pumps and screens change channels. Nearby scenery supports condition checks without rewards. Nine districts have public service stations; all 23 roof lift landings have maintenance panels. Four rare transmissions contain unique lore and grant $45/60 XP each, once per save. Doors retain physical collision and swing protection. The v0.10.0 interiors add furnished skyscraper floors; broad inspection still does not make every object destructible.

Existing credits/XP, usable parts, water, recycling, field readings, completed jobs and claimed rare finds save locally. If storage denies a write, the game reports that progress remains in the current session and retries saving later; a restart can only recover successfully persisted progression. Returning home keeps the current session’s work. The current contract is regenerated when starting a session.

## Rebuild

For the Mac game, run `Unity/BuildMac.command`. It rebuilds the bundled image/audio codec through `Unity/Native/BuildCodec.command` before invoking Unity. Rebuilding that plugin requires Apple Command Line Tools (`xcrun clang` and `codesign`); playing the app does not. Unity’s build menu and direct batch method use the already compiled plugin. See `Unity/README.md` for details.

`SlopCity.Editor.SlopCityRealismValidation.RenderCapture` produces 13 saved-scene views at 1600×1000, numbered `renders/13–25-*.png`, including street, habitat, subway, vehicle, rooftop, civilian and security views. Run it with graphics enabled after rebuilding the scene; the full batch command is in `Unity/README.md`. Captures are review artifacts, not screenshots of a running standalone player.

The optional native `--slop-graphics-review` launch flag records actual player screenshots and timing, then returns to the couch paused and restores saved progression. It is only enabled by that flag; normal launches do not run the review. See `Unity/README.md` for output locations and scope.

To regenerate the separate Blender lounge source, run Blender’s executable with:

```sh
blender --background --factory-startup --python scripts/build_scene.py
blender --background --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python scripts/validate_assets.py
```

On this machine the executable is `../.tools/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender`. The generator overwrites this kit’s native scene, renders, and exports; save manual edits under a different name before rebuilding.
