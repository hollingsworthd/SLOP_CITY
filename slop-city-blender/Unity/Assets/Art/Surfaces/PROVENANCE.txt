UNITY IMPORT AND DERIVATIVES
Albedo textures use sRGB. Normal, occlusion and metallic/smoothness data are linear.
normalGL maps are the provided tangent-space OpenGL/+Y maps. They are imported with
TextureImporterType.NormalMap, without height-to-normal conversion or a green flip.
SlopCitySurfaceLibrary generates *_metallicSmoothness.png from each roughness map:
R = a supplied metalness map for metal materials, or zero for dielectric surfaces;
GB = zero; alpha = 1 minus roughness. Painted/rusted areas remain nonmetallic.
The package's denim metalness scan is preserved as a source but intentionally not used
for the textile material, which is physically a dielectric.
NeutralWearV1, RestrainedRustV1 and NeutralConcreteV1 albedo maps are deterministic
derivatives generated by SlopCitySurfaceLibrary from the identified source albedo.
Neutral wear retains a low-contrast grayscale version of photographed variation so
authored steel/enamel colors remain visible; restrained rust retains 12 percent of
the original rusty colors mixed with a subdued gray base. Neutral concrete removes
the scan's moss/brown palette for indoor platform and ceiling finishes.
*_metal204_metallicSmoothness maps assign 0.8 metalness to exposed steel; *_metal0_
maps make coated enamel dielectric. Their alpha remains one minus measured roughness.
The derived maps retain the CC0 status of the source. No generative images are used.
All normal and packed maps are capped at 1024 pixels with mipmaps and anisotropic
filtering. Material UV scale is derived from measured metres, intended for metre-space
UVs on procedurally built city geometry. Authored lounge model UVs remain authored.
The supplied .hdr is genuine unclipped radiance data with visible daytime clouds;
SlopCitySurfaceLibrary.GetSkyCubemap imports it as an HDR cubemap for sky/reflections.
